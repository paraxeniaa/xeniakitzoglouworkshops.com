Xenia Kitzoglou

About

Xenia (Xeni) Kitzoglou graduated from the Department of Audiovisual Arts at Ionian University in 2024. With experience facilitating art workshops across community and academic settings in Ireland, Greece and Malta, their practice centres on performance and digital art. Xenia is currently enrolled in the MSc in Art Therapy at Crawford College of Art and Design, Cork.
